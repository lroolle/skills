## Saturated aesthetic lanes

If a design lands here without a specific brief reason, it is the
second-order training reflex.

### Editorial-typographic

Display serif (often italic) + small mono labels + ruled separators +
monochromatic restraint. Klim-influenced. Fingerprint: three
rule-separated columns, italic serif headline, lowercase track-spaced
metadata, no imagery. By 2026 every Stripe-adjacent and Notion-adjacent
brand has landed here.

### SaaS-cream

Warm off-white + one accent + rounded cards + Inter or DM Sans + hero
with subtitle and two buttons. Default output of AI landing page
generators.

### Neon-terminal

Dark background + bright monospace + green or amber accent + CRT
scanlines. Was novel 2023, now default "developer tool" aesthetic.

## Saturated domain-color combos

If guessable from the domain name alone, it is the first reflex.

| Domain | Reflex | Try instead |
|---|---|---|
| Healthcare | Teal + white | Any other restrained palette |
| Finance | Navy + gold | Warm, brutalist, or achromatic |
| AI / ML | Purple gradient dark | Literally anything else |
| Crypto | Neon on black | See above |
| Education | Blue + orange | Committed single-color |
| Food | Warm + script font | Swiss, industrial |
| Real estate | Dark blue + gold | Warm ground, gallery |

## Saturated copy

AI prose signals:

Elevate, seamless, robust, delve, empower, unleash, next-gen,
game-changer, cutting-edge, innovative, revolutionize, streamline,
leverage (as verb), curate, synergy, holistic, ecosystem, paradigm shift

Also: em dashes as sentence structure, triadic rhythm (X, Y, and Z
repeated), negation pivots ("Not just X, but Y").

## Update procedure

1. Review AI-generated output across generators (v0, Bolt, etc.).
2. Patterns in >30% of output for a category: add to saturated list.
3. Entries older than 18 months below saturation: remove.
4. Only this file changes. Principles, patterns, materials: stable.
